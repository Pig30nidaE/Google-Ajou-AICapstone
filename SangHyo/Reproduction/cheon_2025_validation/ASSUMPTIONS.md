# Operational assumptions (items the paper does not report)

Each entry: what the paper leaves open, the operational choice made here, why, its expected effect,
and which experiments it applies to. None of these choices is presented anywhere as the paper's
method. Choices were fixed **before** any experiment was run (this file predates `results/`).

## Consequences that are *not* assumptions

- Cohort, label, 72 features, 5 folds, fold-mean aggregation, LightGBM, SHAP-ordered forward selection
  and the Table-2 hyperparameters are taken from the paper (see `PAPER_PROTOCOL.md`).
- The dataset contains no missing values in the 72 features, so "imputation fit on the training
  fold" is vacuous in G/N; this is stated rather than silently claimed as a leakage safeguard.
