# Result comparison — R (reproduction) vs G (subject-independent) vs N (nested CV)

Numbers in the tables at the end are generated by `report_tables.py` from `results/*/metrics.json`
(primary statistic: per-repeat mean over the 5 folds, then mean ± SD and 95 % t-CI over the 5 repeats,
seeds 42–46; A21). Stage names are shared across experiments so the same recipe can be read across columns.

## Identical components (shared code, byte-identical between R, G and N)

- Cohort: AI-Hub Training + Validation pooled, 174 subjects, 12,183 daily records (`src/cheon/data.py`).
- Label: CN = 0 vs MCI + Dem = 1 (7,737 / 4,446 records).
- Record construction: row-wise pairing of the activity and sleep tables (A03); no duplicate handling.
- The 72 features of Figure 2 built deterministically per record (`src/cheon/features.py`): raw scalars,
  clock-hour conversion of bedtime start/end, sleep_time (A07), 12 MET statistics (A08), 4 + 4 count features.
- No imputation, no scaling (there are no missing values; A13).
- Model family: LightGBM (scikit-learn API), `random_state = seed`, single-threaded.
- Hyperparameter sets: library defaults for the base stage; Table 2 (min_data_in_leaf 41, num_leaves 330,
  n_estimators 1000, learning_rate 0.08) for the tuned stage.
- SHAP-importance ranking procedure (TreeExplainer, mean |SHAP|) and the forward-selection path
  (default LightGBM at each k, argmax of the fold-mean AUC; A14, A16).
- Metrics (Accuracy, ROC-AUC, macro Precision/Recall, F1-macro + auxiliaries), decision threshold
  (model default), aggregation rule, seeds.
- 5 folds, 5 repeats; `StratifiedKFold`/`StratifiedGroupKFold` both with `shuffle=True, random_state=seed`.

## Required changes for leakage prevention (R → G)

The algorithmic recipe is the same, but every learned step is fit on the training fold only so that the
test fold stays independent. Concretely:

| Step | R (paper-faithful) | G | Why it had to move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split unit | records; the same subject's days fall on both sides of every fold (the `subject_overlap` column of `fold_metrics.csv` records how many subjects are shared: 174 of 174 in R) | subjects; `subject_overlap` asserted = 0 in every fold | the defining change |
| SHAP ranking | fit + explained on all 12,183 records once per repeat, then the 5 folds are scored | fit + explained on the 9,704-ish training records of each fold | a ranking computed on the test subjects' days would leak their information into the feature set |
| Choice of k | argmax of the record-level CV curve (paper: 40) | fixed a priori at the paper's 40 | searching k on the outer folds would use the test folds; searching it honestly needs an inner loop (= N) |
| Hyperparameters | Table 2 fixed | Table 2 fixed | no change (constants) |
| Imputation / scaling | none | none | nothing to move |

So G is **not** "one splitter line changed": the ranking scope also moved. Two stage pairs are, however,
pure splitter-only comparisons because they involve no learned selection at all:
`baseline_lgbm_default_all72` and `final_tuned_all72`. The recipe pairs `fs_default_k40` and
`final_tuned_k40` additionally carry the ranking-scope change.

## Required changes for selection isolation (G → N)

| Step | G | N | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choice of k | fixed 40 (from the paper) | forward path on 5 inner `StratifiedGroupKFold` folds inside each outer training set; k* = argmax of the inner-mean AUC | the paper's 40 was itself chosen on all subjects (record-level CV); N re-derives it from the outer-training subjects only |
| Hyperparameters | Table 2 fixed | chosen per outer fold on the inner folds from the 16-point paper-anchored grid (A20); the fixed-Table-2 variant (N-A) and the library-default variant are evaluated on the same outer folds | isolates hyperparameter choice from the outer test subjects; N-A shows the effect of nesting only k |
| Outer ranking | training fold | outer training set (same scope as G) | — |
| Threshold | model default | model default (not tuned) | — |

## Reading the tables

**R reproduces the paper.** The Table-1 LightGBM row is reproduced to within 0.0001 ROC-AUC (0.9009 vs
0.9010) and within 0.002 on all five metrics; all seven base models land within 0.003 of the paper's
ROC-AUC except logistic regression (+0.012). The SHAP-ordered forward path selects k* ∈ {47, 35, 44, 41, 33}
(paper 40) with fold-mean AUC 0.9050 ± 0.0006 (paper 0.9037); the curve pooled over repeats peaks at
k = 41 (0.9042) and its plateau for k ≥ 30 spans 0.9009–0.9042, exactly the 0.900–0.904 band of Figure 4.
The Table-2 hyperparameters give 0.9500 ± 0.0006 at k = 40 and 0.9497 at k* (paper 0.9492). The seed-42
SHAP ranking shares 14 of the 20 features of Figure 6, and the selected top-40 contains all 20.
The top-10 ranking is identical across the five seeds.

**G removes the signal.** With subjects held out, the same 72 features, the same fixed k = 40, the same
Table-2 hyperparameters and the same fold definition give ROC-AUC 0.5062 ± 0.0168 [0.4854, 0.5271]
(baseline default model: 0.5088 [0.4836, 0.5340]). Every G stage's 95 % CI contains 0.5 (one-sample t-test
of the five repeat means against 0.5: p = 0.45 for the final model, 0.39 for the baseline); per-fold AUCs
range 0.38–0.60. Within G, neither selection nor tuning changes anything (paired differences vs the
baseline: +0.004 [−0.002, +0.009], −0.003 [−0.011, +0.006], −0.003 [−0.011, +0.005]). The R→G deltas are
−0.392 (baseline) and −0.444 (final model). Because `baseline_lgbm_default_all72` and `final_tuned_all72`
involve no learned selection at all, those two deltas are attributable to the split unit alone: in R every
fold shares all 174 subjects between training and test (`subject_overlap` = 174), in G it is 0.

**N does not recover anything.** With k and hyperparameters chosen inside the outer-training subjects, the
primary result is 0.4851 ± 0.0268 [0.4518, 0.5184]; the fixed-Table-2 variant N-A gives 0.4907 [0.4618,
0.5195]. G and N share identical outer folds (same `StratifiedGroupKFold` seeds), so the paired difference
N − G over the 25 outer folds is −0.021 [−0.052, +0.010] (N wins 9/25) for the primary and −0.016
[−0.043, +0.012] for N-A: no evidence that either selection step helps or hurts beyond noise. The inner
loop shows why: the inner-CV AUC at the selected (k*, H*) averages 0.536 while the outer AUC of the same
choice is 0.485 (selection optimism +0.051); k* is unstable (1–70, median 5, ≤ 5 in 13 of 25 folds) and
the paper's 1000-tree configuration is selected in only 3 of 25 folds.

**Aggregation sensitivity.** Pooled-fold means equal the repeat-level means to four decimals in every
stage (T6); only the SDs differ (fold-level SD ≈ 0.06 for G/N vs repeat-level SD ≈ 0.02). Pooled-OOF AUC
per repeat is within 0.001 of the fold-mean AUC everywhere (T3–T4).
